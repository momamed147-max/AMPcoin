Settle the sign-in card background

Four separate sheets set `background` - the shorthand - on .auth-card:
AuthPage.css, ui-polish.css, theme.css and ui/v3.css. A shorthand resets every
background longhand beneath it, so no amount of source order or specificity could
settle which background actually painted. That is why the card, specifically,
kept reading as the old generic panel while the page behind it and the button
in front of it changed correctly.

One targeted !important on that single declaration ends the argument. Nothing
else on the card needs it - border, shadow and backdrop blur were all applying
normally the whole time, which is the signature of a rule winning on most
properties and losing on one.

Also removes the now-superseded background-image longhand it replaced, so there
is one authoritative declaration rather than two in a row.
