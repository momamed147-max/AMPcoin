Re-grid the lobby rows and re-measure the page

Colour was not the problem and I kept treating it as if it were.

The layout was this: page content stretched edge to edge, so on a 1600px screen
a lobby row came out ~1500px wide holding about 300px of content. The eye
travelled a very long way from "who" to "what" to "how much" and everything
floated in the middle with nothing anchoring it. No amount of gradient fixes
that.

What changed:

- The lobby row is a real three-column grid - who is betting | what is staked |
  what it is worth and what you can do about it - with explicit tracks, dividers
  between the columns, and a tinted background on the outer two. Previously it
  was a flex strip that happened to end with a large gap.
- The value moved into the action column, where you look when deciding whether
  to join. It had been a floating sibling, then briefly in the wager header,
  and printed twice at one point. It is in one place now, full width.
- Join and View are stacked full-width buttons rather than a wide button next
  to a small link, so the secondary no longer reads as an afterthought.
- The content column has a real measure (1180px) instead of running to the edge
  of the display. max-width on .page-content did nothing because it is a flex
  child that grows, so the cap is on the inner column.
- Proportion: avatars 42 to 52px, tiles 40 to 52px, wider name/value columns.
  Everything had been sized for a phone and never scaled up.
- Chat column trimmed 320px to 268px. It said "No messages yet" most of the time
  while taking a third of the screen.

The players column went vertical on the first attempt, which read as a list
rather than a matchup - it is a row again.

css-palette.js asserted "v3.css is the last stylesheet imported". That was true
until three override layers were added after it deliberately. It now asserts
both directions: v3 comes after the layers it must outrank, and the override
layers come after v3, or they do nothing.

415 tests pass across 14 suites.
